Privacy policy

BreachSix: Tactical Squad does not require an account and does not collect personal data on its own servers, because it has none. This page explains what stays on your device, what advertising partners may process, and how to exercise your rights.

In short

  • BreachSix Games has no accounts, no game server and no database.
  • Your scores, skins and preferences are saved on your device and are never sent to us.
  • The game shows ads provided by third-party partners, who may use advertising identifiers or cookies under their own policies.
  • On the website, advertising cookies are enabled only after you agree.
  • The game has no in-app purchases.

1. Who is responsible?

The publisher of the game is BreachSix Games, an independent studio based in Québec (Canada). For any question about this policy or your information, write to breachsixgames@gmail.com. This address is also that of the person in charge of answering privacy questions.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the game saves some information directly on your device. It is never sent to BreachSix Games or to any third party, except in the case described in section 3:

  • your best scores for each level;
  • the weapon skins and karambits you have unlocked;
  • the composition of your squads;
  • your preferences: language, music, sound effects, colorblind mode, larger text, your cookie choice.

This information is kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage). If you play in Telegram, it is also copied to the Telegram app's persistent storage (DeviceStorage) to avoid the progress losses that are typical of mini-apps. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes it permanently: there is no online backup and no synchronization between devices.

On Android, the advertising ID is the only data we declare to Google Play as collected and shared, for advertising purposes; receiving personalized ads is optional.

The ads you trigger yourself (to obtain a weapon skin) are optional. No ad is needed to progress in the game.

4. Cookies and consent

On the website (breachsixgames.com), a banner asks you to accept or refuse advertising cookies:

  • If you refuse, no Google AdSense advertising script is loaded and the game works exactly the same.
  • If you accept, Google AdSense is loaded and may use cookies in accordance with its policy.
  • Your choice is remembered on your device. You can change it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser: the banner will reappear.

The banner appears neither in the Android app nor in Telegram, where advertising is managed by the platform concerned and by the partners listed in the table above.

The site's information pages (guide, universe, FAQ, behind the scenes) use no advertising cookies and no audience measurement tools.

5. Controlling personalized advertising

  • Android: in your device settings, under Google › Ads, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization.
  • Google account: adssettings.google.com lets you manage Google's ad personalization.
  • How Google uses data: how Google uses data from partner sites and apps.
  • Industry opt-out tools: aboutads.info (North America) and youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires a consent message to be shown before personalized ads are served. We are integrating this message into the app.

6. Hosting and platforms

The breachsixgames.com website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may record technical information about visits (for example the IP address) under its own policy. If you install the game from Google Play, or play it in Telegram, the policies of Google and Telegram also apply to your use of those services. BreachSix Games has no access to your Google or Telegram account information.

7. In-app purchases

The game currently offers no in-app purchases and no financial transactions. We process no payment data.

8. Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. It knowingly collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child under 13 is using it, write to us.

9. Your rights (Québec's Law 25 and other applicable laws)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is generally nothing to access, correct or delete at BreachSix Games. You can erase your game data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser.

Depending on where you live (Québec, Canada, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, etc.), you have rights over the personal information processed by advertising partners: access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, objection to personalization. You can exercise them directly with these providers, through the links in the table in section 3, or write to us at breachsixgames@gmail.com: we will tell you how to proceed and pass on the request where possible.

In Québec, you can also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information.

10. Transfers outside Québec

The advertising partners and hosts mentioned above are international companies. The information they process may therefore be processed outside Québec and Canada, under their own policies.

11. Security and retention

Because we keep no data on servers, there is no database on our side that could be compromised. Local data stays on your device for as long as you do not delete it.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated as the game evolves (new partners, new features). The date of the last update appears at the top of the page. We invite you to check it from time to time.

13. Contact us

For any question: breachsixgames@gmail.com. See also the terms of use.
